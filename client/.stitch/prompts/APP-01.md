The entry page for businesses applying to run their own white-label payments portal on ELOKA. It explains what the application needs before they start.

**PLATFORM:** Web, Mobile-first

**PAGE STRUCTURE:**
1. **Top bar:** "ELOKA" wordmark on the left and "Help" on the right.
2. **Intro:** Title "Run your own payments platform", with one plain line: "Apply in one sitting. Your progress is saved, so you can come back any time."
3. **What you'll need:** A simple checklist card titled "Keep these ready":
   - Business PAN
   - The signatory's Aadhaar-linked mobile for DigiLocker
   - Business documents (address proof and registration certificate)
   - Bank account proof
   - Your logo (optional)
4. **Time note:** A small line: "Takes about 20 minutes."
5. **Actions:** A full-width primary button "Start application", and a secondary text link "Continue an existing application".
6. **Footer:** "Privacy" and "Terms" links.

No hero illustration, no statistics, no testimonials, no feature-card grids.

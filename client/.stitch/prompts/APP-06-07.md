Two onboarding steps that use the standard step layout (top bar, "Step N of 13", step title, form card, sticky primary button): Business PAN (step 4) and Business details (step 5).

**PLATFORM:** Web, Mobile-first

**FRAME A — Business PAN:**
1. Title "Business PAN".
2. A labelled field "PAN of the business". It is uppercase, 10 characters, with the placeholder "ABCDE1234F". Helper text: "For a Private Limited company the 4th letter is C."
3. Primary button "Verify PAN".
4. **Pending variant:** an info card "Checking with the income tax records…" with a small progress indicator and a "Check again" link.
5. **Success variant:** a success card with a definition list:
   - "PAN": XXXXX1234X
   - "Name as per PAN": SAMPLE TRADERS PRIVATE LIMITED
   - "Holder type": Company

   Then a "Continue" button.
6. **Error variants**, shown as inline messages:
   - "We couldn't verify this PAN. 2 attempts left."
   - "This PAN belongs to an individual. Enter the company's PAN."
   - "This PAN is already registered with ELOKA. Contact support."

**FRAME B — Business details** (long form, grouped into three cards):
1. **"Business" card:** Business name; Company type; GSTIN (optional); CIN (optional).
2. **"Signatory and contact" card:** Authorised signatory name; Contact mobile (+91); Alternate mobile (optional); signature upload, shown as a dropzone "Upload signature (JPG, PNG or PDF, up to 10 MB)" with an uploaded-file row variant.
3. **"Address" card:** Address line 1; Address line 2 (optional); District; State (select); Pincode (6 digits).
4. Every field has its label above. One field shows an error example: "Enter a valid 6-digit pincode".
5. An info note variant: "Your business name differs from the PAN record. Our team will check this."
6. Sticky primary button "Save and continue".

**Desktop version of Frame B:** a 280px step rail on the left listing all 13 steps (4 completed, step 5 current); the form column on the right at max 720px; two-column field pairs where sensible.

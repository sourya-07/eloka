MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT - ELOKA PLATFORM

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between ELOKA Technologies Inc. and the applicant executing this document.

1. Definitions

"Platform" means the proprietary financial and identity verification web ecosystem managed by ELOKA. "Services" refers to credit evaluation, compliance logging, and secure data handling provided during onboarding.

2. Services

The applicant agrees to provide accurate, verifiable documentation. ELOKA reserves the right to re-verify records periodically to maintain institutional regulatory standards and prevent fraudulent activities across node clusters.

3. Fees

Processing fees are non-refundable once background checks are initiated. Institutional rates apply as outlined in the supplementary schedule provided during step 4.
